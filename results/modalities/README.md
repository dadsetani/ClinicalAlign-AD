# Stage 2: multimodal merge and QC

These files are aggregate, non-identifying summaries generated by:

    python scripts/02_build_multimodal_dataset.py

The participant-level output is written to the Git-ignored file
`data/derived/multimodal_cohort.csv`.

Selection is anchored to each participant's diagnosis-defined baseline. Clinical
records may occur from 180 days before to 90 days after baseline; plasma NfL,
DTI, and FreeSurfer records may occur from 365 days before to 90 days after
baseline. No selected record may occur on or after the participant's outcome
date.

DTI uses complete robust-mean V2/V3 scans as the primary source and complete V1
scans for older phases. Standard-mean V2/V3 summaries are retained separately
for sensitivity analysis. Scans with severe artifacts, failed conversion,
incomplete acquisition, poor field of view, or failed atlas registration are
excluded. Other QC notes are retained as warnings.

FreeSurfer records with an overall `Fail` rating are excluded. Regional QC
failures mask affected regional features, and `Hippocampus Only` records retain
only hippocampal measurements.
